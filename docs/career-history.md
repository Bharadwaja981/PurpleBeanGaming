# Career history

Public careers use stable profile slugs, never auth UUIDs. Draft identity, team, Tournament MMR, price, and captain identity come from immutable Phase 05 snapshots; placements come from Phase 06 result snapshots; match and rating history come from canonical matches and the versioned ledger. Current profile, Tournament MMR, team, or organization branding changes do not rewrite those captured tournament values.

Career totals include tournaments entered/completed, distinct historical teams, championships, runner-up finishes, match record, auction history, rating/confidence, achievements, and recent form. Forfeits remain visible and marked in match history but are excluded from Competitive Rating. Superseded matches are excluded.
