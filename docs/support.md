# Support operations

Users can open tickets, view their own tickets, post public replies, and close their tickets. Support agents can work the queue and add internal notes. Internal messages are excluded by row-level security from requester reads.

Support membership does not grant access to moderation cases, evidence, sanctions, platform configuration, or rating administration. Ticket closure is audited. Rate limits protect ticket creation without affecting the live auction bid path.

Requesters can open a ticket, read public replies, reply, close, and reopen a resolved/closed ticket for seven days. Staff can assign, prioritize, publish a reply, add a private internal note, and move through the supported statuses. RLS removes internal notes from requester queries and page output.
