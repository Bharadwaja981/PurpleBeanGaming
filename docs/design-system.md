# Purple Bean design system

The product uses a dense, professional dark-esports interface derived from the approved mockups. The canvas is navy-black (`#050914`), operational surfaces are navy (`#10192c` and `#131e34`), borders are cool blue-gray (`#293653`), and violet (`#b535f6`) is reserved for brand emphasis and primary actions. Semantic colors remain green for success, amber for warnings, coral-red for danger, and cyan for information.

Global navigation uses the brand lockup, compact primary links, search, and authentication controls. Tournament and platform workspaces use an integrated 220 px sidebar on desktop and a horizontally scrollable section bar on smaller screens. Do not create navigation destinations without real routes.

Cards use thin borders, 10 px radii, minimal shadow, and compact 16–24 px padding. Tables retain rows rather than becoming oversized cards; headings are small uppercase labels, separators are subtle, and numeric content stays aligned. Buttons use a violet primary fill, bordered navy secondary treatment, and red only for destructive actions. Inputs share navy backgrounds, purple focus rings, visible labels, and minimum 42 px interaction height.

At 900 px the workspace sidebar becomes a sticky horizontal navigation bar. At 560 px branding compacts, page padding reduces, and dense tables remain in explicit horizontal overflow containers. All controls require visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, adequate contrast, and reduced-motion support.
