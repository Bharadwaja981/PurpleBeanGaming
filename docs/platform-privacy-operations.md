# Platform privacy and retention

PurpleBeanGaming separates mutable personal profile data from legitimate historical tournament records. Account deactivation blocks future operational participation while preserving completed competition integrity. Privacy access/deletion requests are tracked for review rather than triggering unsafe automatic deletion.

Retention metadata documents public history, audits, moderation evidence, support tickets, and account data. Automatic deletion is disabled until an approved legal policy and deletion job exist. Moderation evidence and support internal notes are private.

Audit views render an allowlist of fields and never expose tokens, passwords, secrets, or private evidence locations. Security events and abuse signals are visible only to authorized operations staff and are not automatic enforcement decisions.

Account deactivation requires the literal `DEACTIVATE` confirmation. It disables future operational participation without deleting identity-linked historical results. Reactivation is deferred to a future identity-verified support process; there is intentionally no weak self-service recovery path.
