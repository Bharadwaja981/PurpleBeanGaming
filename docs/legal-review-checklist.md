# Professional legal review checklist

Professional review is pending. Before public launch, qualified counsel must approve:

- launch jurisdiction, controller identity, and contact details;
- governing law, dispute, warranty, and liability terms;
- age/minor eligibility and parental-consent requirements;
- precise retention periods and deletion/data-request language;
- processor and subprocessor inventory, regions, and transfer safeguards;
- cookie and browser-storage disclosures;
- OpenDota, Steam, Valve, Supabase, Vercel, and monitoring disclosures;
- tournament moderation, sanctions, appeals, evidence, and historical-integrity practices;
- security/privacy incident notification obligations;
- effective dates, versioning, and material-change notification.

Do not remove the draft notices from the public policy pages until this checklist has an accountable reviewer, approval date, jurisdiction, and recorded policy version.
