# Draft history

An organizer creates one idempotent snapshot after every team has its captain plus the required drafted recruits and the tournament reaches `rosters_locked`, `competition`, or `completed`.

Snapshots retain tournament rules, team identity, captain display identity, roster identity, `tournament_mmr_at_draft`, purchase price, credits, bid counts, and completion totals. Later profile, display-name, or current-rating changes do not rewrite historical values.

Snapshot tables reject anonymous and authenticated direct mutations. The organizer-only RPC is the sole client entry point.

Auction history filtering and pagination run in PostgreSQL. Filters cover player, nominating or winning team, status, price range, nomination round, and normal versus unsold-round acquisition. Captain profiles aggregate immutable team snapshots across tournaments. Organizer CSV downloads contain only the documented snapshot fields and are generated by an authorization-checked database function.
